    Welcome! Here is how it works

    Oranje Uurwerk means “Orange Clockwork”. Uurwerk is the Dutch word for a clock, and it also sounds like “hour work”. That is exactly what we do here: one good hour of work at a time.

    ⏱️The 60 / 10 / 40 rhythm

    1. Focus for 60 minutes. Pick one task and give it your full attention.
    2. Take a 10-minute short break. Stand up, drink water, look out of the window.
    3. After 4 focus rounds, take a 40-minute long break. Eat something, go for a walk, rest your eyes.

    You can change all of these times in Settings. Choose what works for you.

    ▶️Using the timer

    • Press START to begin. Press it again to pause. The space bar works too.
    • Press the arrow button to finish a round early and go to the next one.
    • Press the round arrow to restart the round.
    • When a round ends, the Dutch national anthem, the Wilhelmus, plays for you.
    • Add your tasks below the timer. Tap a task to make it your current task.

    🗺️Three levels, 36 hours

    Every full hour of focus unlocks something new. Every minute counts, even if you stop a round early.

    Level 1: ProvincesHours 1 to 12. Unlock all 12 provinces on the map.
    Level 2: Dutch iconsHours 13 to 24. Meet 12 famous Dutch people.
    Level 3: InnovationsHours 25 to 36. Discover 12 Dutch inventions.

    You also get one gift every hour, like a stroopwafel or a pair of clogs. You can see them all in Nederland.

    📈Grow the country

    While you focus, the Netherlands gets better: its HDI, quality of life, healthcare, safety, education, tourism, population and GDP all go up, live.

    After 36 hours, the Netherlands becomes the best country in the world to live in. That is the end of the game, and a big celebration. You can keep using the timer after that.

    💡Tips for a good hour

    • Put your phone in another room, or face down.
    • Write the task down before you start. Small tasks are easier to begin.
    • If your mind wanders, that is normal. Just come back to the task.
    • Really rest in your breaks. A rested brain works better.

    🔋Keep the timer on time

    • You can switch tabs or minimise the window. The timer uses the real clock, so it stays correct.
    • Leave “Keep timer awake” on in Settings. It plays a silent sound so your browser does not slow the page down.
    • Turn on notifications in Settings to get a message when a round ends.
    • Do not close the tab while a round is running. If you do, your time is still saved and the timer continues when you come back.

    💾Your progress is safe

    Your hours, tasks and settings are saved in this browser on this device. No account is needed.

    Want to move to another computer? Go to Settings and use Save backup, then Load backup on the other device.
